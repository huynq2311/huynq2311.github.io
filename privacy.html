Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who uses the app

"Wondipix uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Wondipix YouTube channel, on their own computer. It is not offered to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, through the YouTube API Services, the app:

  • uploads the channel's own videos and sets their titles, descriptions and schedules;
  • reads the channel's own videos and basic account details, to check it uses the right channel;
  • reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers).

How the data is stored and shared

Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain of the owner's computer. Statistics are stored in local files on that computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or sent to any server other than Google's.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
